## 🏛️ About EUR-UN

EUR-UN is an independent open-source engineering collective dedicated to collaborative maintenance, infrastructure tooling, and applied systems projects.

We maintain community distributions with explicit upstream attribution, develop research and tooling initiatives, and incubate software that benefits from shared governance rather than single-maintainer ownership.

## 📐 Engineering Principles

- **Evidence over claims**: Engineering claims require measurable, verifiable proof.
- **Upstream attribution**: Honor original authors, maintainers, and licenses transparently.
- **Small, reviewable changes**: Prefer modular, auditable pull requests over monolithic refactors.
- **Security by default**: Enforce least privilege, secrets isolation, and dependency supply chain auditing.
- **Maintainability over novelty**: Build software designed to be reliably maintained for years.
